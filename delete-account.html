Account & Data Deletion

Anna Williams | آنا وليامز app — Cosmetics Store
Developer: Dianah Sayud · App ID: com.annawilliams.app

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Anna Williams app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Select Account & Privacy → Delete Account
  4. Confirm the request

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request it from us

If you cannot access the app, contact us from the email address or phone number registered on your account and state that you are requesting account deletion. We complete verified requests within 7 business days.

WhatsApp: +964 775 555 7950 Email: anna.williams.eve@gmail.com

Data that is permanently deleted

  • Login account (email and password)
  • Profile: name, phone number, delivery address
  • Shopping cart and saved items
  • Your product reviews and ratings
  • Support chats and messages
  • Notifications and back-in-stock alerts
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

Data that is retained — anonymized

Completed order records, aggregated usage statistics and coupon redemption records are kept permanently disconnected from your account — no longer linked to your name, email, phone or address — for accounting, tax and legal purposes. They cannot be traced back to you as an individual.

Retention period

  • Your personal data: deleted immediately on confirmation. No additional retention period, no grace period, no delayed copies.
  • Anonymized records: kept indefinitely, but they are no longer personal data once disconnected from your account.
  • Backups: your data may persist in encrypted backups for a maximum of 30 days before automatic rotation. Backups are accessed only to restore the system after a technical failure.
